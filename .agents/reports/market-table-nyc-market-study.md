### 2.2 Competitive Gaps Identified

After analyzing the competitive set, the following **gaps exist** in Midtown that The Market Table can exploit:

1. **No true Modern Casual with elevated brunch** — Brunch in Midtown is dominated by hotel restaurants (The Parliament, Grand Brasserie) or old-school haunts (Fresco). There is a gap for a **neighborhood-focused Modern Casual brunch with photogenic dishes**.

2. **No strong Happy Hour anchor** — While some have bar programs, none of the nearby competitors market a strong 4-7PM Happy Hour. This is the #1 diner trend for 2026.

3. **Missing "local charm"** — 48% of diners want cozy/local charm. The Parliament = hotel, Le Rock = brasserie, Bar Rocco = celebrity chef. The gap is for a **warm, neighborhood-feeling restaurant with local sourcing**.

4. **Affordable premium** — Midtown is polarized between $50 casual and $100+ fine dining. The gap is **$60-75 average check** — elevated but approachable.

5. **Healthy/light options** — Plant-based items up 45%, mocktails up 52%. Most competitors treat these as afterthoughts. The Market Table can make them **menu centerpieces**.
