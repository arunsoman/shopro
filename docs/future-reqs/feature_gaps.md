# Shopro POS: Feature Gap Analysis (Top 10 Missing Features)

Following a comprehensive competitive analysis of industry leaders (**Toast, Square, Lightspeed, and TouchBistro**), this report identifies the top 10 features Shopro POS currently lacks but are standard or emerging in the 2026 enterprise restaurant market.

---

## 1. AI-Powered Demand & labor Forecasting
- **Competitor Reference**: Square for Restaurants, Toast.
- **The Gap**: While Shopro has basic analytics, it lacks **predictive modeling**. Competitors use historical data (weather, holidays, events) to predict how many staff members are needed and which ingredients will spike in demand.
- **Value**: Reduces labor waste and prevents "stock-outs" before they happen.

## 2. AI Invoice Processing (OCR Scanning)
- **Competitor Reference**: Toast (xtraCHEF).
- **The Gap**: Shopro's "3-Way Match" is powerful, but it requires manual entry of invoice data. Competitors allow staff to take a photo of a paper invoice, which AI converts into digital inventory records automatically.
- **Value**: Saves hours of back-office data entry and reduces human error.

## 3. Omnichannel Delivery Sync (Marketplace Middleware)
- **Competitor Reference**: Lightspeed, Toast.
- **The Gap**: Shopro has researched delivery flows, but lacks a "plug-and-play" integration with **Deliverect** or **Otter**. This means "Tablet Hell" (multiple tablets for different apps) still exists for the operator.
- **Value**: Consolidates UberEats, DoorDash, and others into the main Shopro KDS with bi-directional menu/stock sync.

## 4. Native Online Reservation Engine
- **Competitor Reference**: TouchBistro (TouchBistro Reservations).
- **The Gap**: Shopro has an excellent Floor Plan and Waitlist, but lacks an **external-facing booking portal** for guests to reserve tables from the restaurant's website or Google Maps.
- **Value**: Captures guest data earlier in the lifecycle and reduces reliance on expensive third-party platforms like OpenTable.

## 5. Advanced Labor Management & Tip Pooling
- **Competitor Reference**: Toast, 7shifts (Square integration).
- **The Gap**: Shopro tracks logins, but lacks complex **tip-pooling algorithms** (e.g., points-based or hour-based distribution) and integrated staff scheduling that prevents overtime.
- **Value**: Essential for larger restaurants and chains to remain labor-law compliant and keep staff happy with transparent earnings.

## 6. Integrated Financial Services (Fintech)
- **Competitor Reference**: Square (Square Capital), Toast (Toast Checking).
- **The Gap**: Major players offer **instant access to sales funds** through branded debit cards or small-business loans based on POS sales history.
- **Value**: Provides critical liquidity to restaurant owners, locking them into the ecosystem.

## 7. Integrated Mobile EMV/NFC Payment SDK
- **Competitor Reference**: Square Terminal, Toast Go 2.
- **The Gap**: Shopro's Flutter apps are great for ordering, but lack native integration with **card-present hardware** (NFC/Chip readers). Payments are currently "Multi-modal" but often require a detached terminal.
- **Value**: Enables true "Pay-at-Table" which increases table turn-over by 15-20%.

## 8. AI-Enabled Voice Ordering (Phone & Drive-Thru)
- **Competitor Reference**: Square.
- **The Gap**: An emerging feature where an AI agent answers the restaurant's phone, takes the order, and injects it directly into the POS/KDS without staff intervention.
- **Value**: Frees up hosts during peak rushes and ensures no phone order is ever missed.

## 9. Dedicated Self-Service Kiosk Mode
- **Competitor Reference**: Square, Toast.
- **The Gap**: While Shopro has tableside QR ordering, it lacks a dedicated "Kiosk Interface" designed for large, stationary touchscreens in Quick Service (QSR) environments.
- **Value**: Reduces front-counter labor costs and typically increases average ticket size through automated upselling prompts.

## 10. Native White-Labeled Guest App
- **Competitor Reference**: Appfront (Toast integration).
- **The Gap**: Shopro uses a web-based QR app for guests. Competitors offer a **native iOS/Android app** that guests can download, which supports push notifications, geofenced offers, and "One-tap reordering."
- **Value**: Higher retention and deeper brand loyalty compared to ephemeral web-based sessions.

---

## Strategic Recommendation
To maintain its "Strategic Procurement" lead, Shopro should prioritize **Gap #2 (AI Invoice Scanning)** first, as it directly complements the existing 3-Way Match logic. Entering the **Fintech (#6)** or **Voice AI (#8)** space should be considered long-term experimental goals.
