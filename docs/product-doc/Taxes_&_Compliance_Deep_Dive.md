# Taxes & Compliance Deep Dive

Shopro's tax engine is a high-precision calculation suite designed to handle complex international tax jurisdictions with multi-dimensional applicability rules.

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Rule Engine
The `TaxEngine.java` evaluates multiple data points for every line item to ensure perfect compliance.

### Applicability Dimensions
- **Temperature-Based**: The system differentiates between `HOT` and `COLD` items (line 143), a requirement for specific regions (like the UK or parts of the US) where hot prepared food carries different tax liabilities than cold items.
- **Consumption Mode**: Rules are toggled based on `DINE_IN` vs. `TAKEAWAY` status (lines 139-140).
- **Price Thresholds**: Support for luxury or "sugar taxes" that only trigger when an item's unit price falls within a specific range (`PriceThresholdMin` / `PriceThresholdMax`).
- **Category Specifics**: Targeted rules for `ALCOHOL`, `TOBACCO`, or `SERVICE` categories.

---

## 2. Advanced Mathematical Models
- **Inclusive vs. Exclusive**: The system handles both "Tax-Inclusive" (GST/VAT style) where tax is backward-calculated from the gross price, and "Tax-Exclusive" (US Sales Tax style) where it is added at the end.
- **Cascading Logic**: Supports "Tax on Tax" scenarios through the `isCascading` flag (line 112), where a secondary tax is calculated on the subtotal plus the primary tax amount.
- **Regional Hard-Coding**: Includes built-in compliance guards like the "Saudi Alcohol Guard" which prevents prohibited items from being processed in specific regions, and "Indian GST Split" for automatic dual-ledger (CGST/SGST) reporting.

---

## 3. Transparency & Verification
Compliance requires visibility. Shopro provides two primary tools for this:

- **Bill Simulator**: A dedicated utility in the `TaxesDashboardPage` that allows admins to "Run Simulation" on a mock order. It displays exactly which rules triggered for which items and provides a detailed `TaxBreakdownEntry` for audit.
- **Tax Audit Logs**: Every calculation result is stored in `TaxAuditLog.java`, creating an immutable trail of tax liabilities generated by the system, categorized by rule code and venue ID.

---

## 4. Service Charge & Surcharges
The engine treats Service Charges as a specialized tax-adjacent calculation. It looks for a specific `SERVICE_CHARGE` rule code and applies the venue's effective rate to a dedicated `serviceChargeAmount` field, keeping it separate from standard item taxes for cleaner accounting.
